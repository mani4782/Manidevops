DevOps emerged as the ultimate solution, building upon Agile's strengths and addressing its remaining challenges.
The core of DevOps is enabling continuous integration and continuous deployment. Developers write code on day one, and it is immediately built, compiled, and tested.
If defects are found, the developer is notified instantly, making them "very easy" to clear due to the recent  code. This drastically reduces the number of invalid defects

The DevOps leads to Speed, Accuracy and business improvement, faster release with fewer defects .
DevOps is defined by continuous improvement and fundamentally welcomes change. Unlike Waterfall and Agile, which struggle with changes, DevOps can "digest" and incorporate new market trends like DevSecOps (integrating security), Shift Left, AI Ops, ML Ops, and GitOps. This inherent adaptability ensures that DevOps will remain relevant "as long as software exists"
